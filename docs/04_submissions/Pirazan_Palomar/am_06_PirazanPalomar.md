# Your Paper
## Task 06.01 - Introduction
_**by: David Leonardo Pirazán Palomar**_

**Write the introduction for your paper.**

This paper analyses communication between plants in the tropical rainforest and its application in interactive art, where plants and humans can form a symbiotic relationship.

Dramaturgy is an essential component of human storytelling, but its anthropocentric approach can be challenged and expanded through post-humanism and eco-feminism. This allows us to identify and/or create ways in which plants can be represented as active agents and characters with feelings, interactions and speech.

Similar to the way soil, water and sunlight contribute to a plant's growth, this paper emerges from a fertile foundation of existing frameworks for more-than-human interaction and a dramaturgical framework for traditional and experimental storytelling. This allows us to define existing ways in which we can create dramaturgies in a co-creative relationship with plants and expand our knowledge of new ways of interacting with and creating alongside them.
