WRAPUP

- **How would you rate the difficulty of this lecture from 1 (far too easy) to 5 (far too difficult)?**

  - **Rating: 4.** I found it demanding, and due to the limited time, also a bit stressful towards the end. I struggled to create a coherent structure and would have needed more time for that. This was mainly due to two factors: first, during the semester my highest priority was to really learn C++ well, so this lecture became a second priority; and second, I had a very specific idea in mind for the paper but lacked sufficient knowledge on the topic.

- **How would you rate the amount of work you had to put into this lecture so far from 1 (no work at all) to 5 (far too much work)?**

  - **Rating: 3.** During the semester, the workload was reasonable, but later it became quite high.

- **Which one was your favorite chapter, and which one your least favorite?**

  - It’s difficult to say, as all topics were very relevant — so this is probably just a personal opinion. I enjoyed _Argumentation & Artistic Research_ the most. I enjoyed _Anatomy of a Paper_ less, as it was already familiar to me, and _Reasoning & Methods_ because I found it quite hard to understand.

- **Do you feel well prepared to write your short paper? If not, what was missing?**

  - I think I will never feel fully prepared — and that’s okay. It doesn’t mean I won’t keep trying my best. The main issue is a lack of experience, as well as the fact that there are so many different approaches and examples that vary greatly. It’s really difficult to know whether I’m on the right track or not.

- **Please feel free to add any feedback you want to give!**
  - I would have enjoyed more lessons on writing style and storytelling. I think this is also very relevant in academic writing, and maybe there are some fun and interesting exercises out there that could be included in the lessons!
